# Rough cut

Understand theme, argument, narrative sequence, repetitions, errors, silence, tangents, and conclusion before cutting. Remove only material whose absence preserves subject, causality, context, and payoff.

Perform semantic rough cut before captions, B-roll, motion graphics, or pacing embellishment. Explain each cut as redundancy, retake, error, unproductive silence, or verified tangent; keep required breath and context.
