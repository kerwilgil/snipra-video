# Quality Gate

Assess independently from 0–3: event importance, event coherence, event completeness, setup quality, escalation, payoff strength, reaction value, temporal continuity, visual novelty, hook potential, highlight potential, montage value, boundary quality, dead time, and menu contamination. Zero means absent, contradicted, or harmful; three means compelling and evidenced. Keep the polarity of dead time and menu contamination visible rather than hiding it in a total.

Decide `KEEP` for a sufficiently evidenced fit, `REWORK` for an event repairable by context, filmstrip, or boundaries, and `REJECT` for irrelevant, contaminated, incoherent, or defeated material. Do not auto-sum the dimensions into a universal score before human validation.
